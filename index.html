ABOUT THE INSTITUTE

Engineering with purpose.

N. K. Orchid College of Engineering & Technology (NKOCET), Solapur, is managed by Pradnya Niketan Education Society, Pune. The institute focuses on technical education, practical learning, innovation and student development.

The college website describes the institute as autonomous, AICTE-approved and NAAC A+ accredited. Please verify current approvals and accreditation status on the official website.
